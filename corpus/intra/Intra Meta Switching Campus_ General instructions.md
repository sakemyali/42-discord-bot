# Intra Meta Switching Campus: General instructions

In order to switch from one campus to another, you need the following:

- you MUST have validated your Common Core, and be part of the 42Advanced (the second part of the curriculum).
- you must ask your local pedagogical team. It can refuse the transfer.
- you must ask the distant pedagogical team. It can also refuse the transfer. Check the campus contact on 42network.org
- you have to define your switch date, close all local projects teams, backup your local home directory.
- you can't be on internship, part-time (or any professional experience) at the switch date
- you have by default to handle any logistics (visa, travel, housing, ...). Ask the distant campus to check if they provide any support.
