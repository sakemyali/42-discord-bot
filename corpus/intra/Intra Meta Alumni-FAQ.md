# Intra Meta Alumni-FAQ

⚠️ For Pushback or Alumnization status inquiries, contact your campus Pedago team directly. Emails to other addresses will not be processed. ⚠️

### What is a 42 Alumni?

The term alumni (singular and plural) is derived from the Latin “alumnus”. It refers to the former students of an institution.

At 42, it designates students from any 42 campuses around the world who have at least completed the Common Core and are no longer pursuing their curriculum.

When you are an Alumni, the message “You're now an alumni!” appears on your intra account.

## ALUMNI STATUS

### Why become an Alumni?

The Alumni status allows students who have either completed or are no longer active in the 42 training to continue being part of the 42 community.

Joining the Alumni community grants benefits (see section Benefits), including access to the 42alumni.org platform and the ability to engage with the 42 community all over the world.

Exception: Alumni from a closed campus (i.e. School 21) no longer have access to the 42 pedagogy. Your intra account is deactivated: you can log in but won’t be able to access anything.

### What are the requirements to become an Alumni?

To become an Alumni, you must have completed the Common Core. The Alumni Launchpad will then appear on your intranet, and your Alumnization countdown will start.

IMPORTANT: the decision to end your training at 42 and to become an Alumni is your responsibility. It is **irrevocable** (can't be undone) and freezes your level permanently. You must therefore consider it carefully. We recommend continuing your training at 42 until you have reached your personal objectives.

If you are on a degree track (e.g. RNCP), make sure that you have completed all the mandatory steps (required level, projects, jury evaluation) before becoming an Alumni.

The Alumni Launchpad (= Spontaneous Alumnization) cannot be activated DURING a company project (work experience, internship, apprenticeship).

The Alumni Launchpad (= Spontaneous Alumnization) can be activated after completing the Common Core and BEFORE starting a work experience. However, we recommend waiting until the end of your work placement to become Alumni with 3 extra levels (e.g. level 12 instead of 9).

After 8 months of inactivity, you will automatically become Alumni. For more information on the Automatic Alumnization (countdown, reminder emails, Pushback), see section What is the Automatic Alumnization?.

**Gain a lifetime access to the 42 intra***: keep your intra profile and history, stay updated on the latest projects. You can still complete projects for personal development but you will no longer earn XP.**Get your official 42 certificates and badge(s)**(Credly) - useful for industry recognition, background checks, visa requests, and more. Only Alumni (and RNCP-holder) can access this service.**Get invited to exclusive events**: Alumni Gala, afterworks, conferences, hackathon and more…**Network with 42 global Alumni community on the Alumni platform**: (re)connect with peers anywhere in the world, discuss topics of interest, find new job opportunities, hear the latest 42 news, and much more…**Access any 42 campuses in the world**(subject to each campus’ conditions).**Shop exclusive merch**on the 42 Shop reserved for Alumni.**Recruit your next talents**during 42 job fairs.**Keep your student email address**(@student.42.fr for example) and therefore the accounts and services associated with it (GitHub, cloud providers, etc.).**Keep your access to the global ‘42born2code’ Slack**. If you can't access it, email alumni@42.frAnd many more!

Alumni remain eligible to obtain an RNCP certification. For more information, go to the dedicated section.

These benefits are not available if you are under an Alumni ban.

### What is an Alumni ban?

Following a disciplinary sanction or exclusion, a campus may declare a student ineligible for Alumni status (no access to Alumni benefits or 42 resources). The student will receive an email notification of this decision, see a mention on their intra account, and can appeal it by contacting the campus Pedago.

## BECOMING AN ALUMNI

### How do I become an Alumni?

It depends on your status:

**You are studying in the Common Core**

You are not yet eligible for the Alumni status. The Alumni Launchpad will appear on your intranet once you complete the Common Core.**You are in the 42Advanced curriculum (post-Common Core)***Spontaneous Alumnization*

You can decide to activate the Alumni Launchpad at any time and become Alumni : https://profile.intra.42.fr/alumni**Caution: it is a definitive action (can't be undone).**

Your level will be permanently frozen but you will still be able to complete projects (without earning XP), access 42 campuses and obtain RNCP certifications as an Alumni.

If you have completed the Common Core but haven’t started your work experience yet, it is possible to activate the Alumni Launchpad but we recommend waiting until the end of your work placement to become Alumni with 3 extra levels.

If you are in the process of validating a company project (work experience, internship or apprenticeship), you are not eligible for Spontaneous Alumnization until it is validated.

If you are not eligible for the Alumni status (i.e haven’t finished the Common Core, have an ongoing company project, are Blackholed, are part of the 42.zip cursus, etc.), the Alumni Launchpad will not appear in your intranet.*Automatic Alumnization (in case of inactivity)*

After 8 months of inactivity (no XP increase), you will automatically become Alumni - applies to all campuses.

As soon as you gain XP, your Alumnization date is postponed by 8 months.

You will receive two reminder emails (including your exact Alumnization date): 4 months before, then 1 month before your Alumnization date. Your Alumnization date is visible at all times on your intranet homepage (v3 only - if you're on v2, switch to v3 to see your Alumnization date).

Alumnization occurs**at 11:00 PM on the scheduled date**(e.g. if your Alumnization deadline is January 10, you will be Alumnized at 11:00 PM of that day).

The maximum time in 42Advanced (post-Common Core) is 4,5 years, after which you will be automatically alumnized.

If you wish to continue your training but are not currently able to earn XP, you can request a Pushback to your campus Pedago**sufficient time before your deadline expires**. Your campus Pedago can extend your Alumnization date by up to 8 additional months from the present date. Caution: Pushback conditions may vary by campus.*Ongoing company project (work experience, internship, apprenticeship ...)*

If you are in the process of validating a company project (work experience, internship, apprenticeship ...), you are not eligible for Spontaneous Alumnization until it is validated.

Automatic Alumnization is postponed for the duration of your company project - this does not prevent you from receiving reminder emails if your Alumnization date is close to the end date of your company project. Reminder: during an apprenticeship, you can earn XP (= Alumnization pushed).

**You are Blackholed**

You are not eligible for the Alumni status.

### What is the Automatic Alumnization? How does it work?

The Automatic Alumnization is in use on all campuses in the 42 network.

Students in the 42Advanced curriculum (post-Common Core) have 8 months to gain XP or start a company project before becoming an Alumni.

- As soon as you gain XP, your Alumnization date is postponed by 8 months.
- During a company project (work experience, internship, apprenticeship), your Alumnization date is postponed for the length of the project. It is impossible to be Alumnized during a company project (your contract need to be uploaded for your company project to be recognized by the system).

You may receive reminder emails during a company project if your work experience end date is close to your Alumnization date. These reminders alert you that you'll have limited time after finishing the company project to earn XP before automatic Alumnization. Reminder: during ‘Apprenticeship’, students can earn XP (= Alumnization postponed).

If you need extra time, or if you are facing a personal situation preventing you from completing projects before your deadline expires, you can request a Pushback to your campus Pedago. Your campus Pedago can extend your Alumnization date by up to 8 additional months from the present date. **Make sure to leave enough time between your Pushback request and your Alumnization date** - avoid last-minute requests! Pushback conditions may vary by campus.

Your Alumnization date is visible in the two reminder emails, and at all times on your intranet homepage (intra v3 only). If you're on intra v2, switch to intra v3 to see your Alumnization date.

Students receive two reminder emails: 4 months, then 1 month before the Alumnization date. Both reminder emails display the scheduled Alumnization date. If you have unsubscribed from 42 emails, you will still receive Alumnization reminders on your 42 student email.

⚠️ Alumnization occurs **at 11:00 PM on the scheduled date** (e.g. if your Alumnization deadline is January 10, you will be Alumnized at 11:00 PM of that day). ⚠️

Alumnization is a definitive action and is not reversible.

The maximum time in 42Advanced is 4,5 (four and a half) years. This "hard deadline" is calculated from the moment you complete ‘Transcendance’ and 'Exam rank 6'. Note: the hard deadline of students who have completed the Common Core before 15 April 2024 is calculated from 11 September 2024 (=> 11 March 2029).

Remember: you don't necessarily have to wait for your Alumnization date to become Alumni. You can spontaneously become Alumni by clicking on the Alumni Launchpad (see section What is the Alumni Launchpad?).

The Alumni Launchpad is the intranet tool allowing you to spontaneously become Alumni.

Once you have completed the Common Core, you can activate the Alumni Launchpad at any time, unless you have an ongoing company project (work experience, internship, apprenticeship ...).

In the intra, click on your login, choose "Settings" then "Alumni" in the menu. Or go to https://profile.intra.42.fr/alumni.

**Caution: it is a definitive action**(can't be undone). Your level will be permanently frozen but you will still be able to complete projects (without earning XP), access 42 campuses and obtain RNCP certifications as an Alumni.Once your Alumni Launchpad is activated: You receive an email confirming your Alumni status and inviting you to complete the Alumni Survey - that's how you "activate your benefits". You receive an email from Credly, the platform to obtain your 42 certificates and badges.

Upon activation of your benefits, you will receive two separate emails to access:

a. The Alumni platform 42alumni.org

b. The 42 Alumni Shop (with a personal discount)

If the Alumni Launchpad does not appear in your intra, you are probably not eligible for the Alumni status (i.e haven’t finished the Common Core, have an ongoing company project, are Blackholed, are part of the 42.zip cursus, etc.).

### When is the best time to become an Alumni?

There is no “good” time, it depends on your personal objectives and current situation. Ask yourself these questions:

**Are you about to start a work experience?**We recommend completing your work experience before becoming an Alumni to gain extra levels. The Alumnization freezes your level permanently.**Are you aiming for a RNCP certification?**It is recommended to get the certification before becoming an Alumni. In case the certification requires you to complete additional projects, it will be easier to validate them as a student than as an Alumni. However, it is still possible to earn a RNCP certification as an Alumni (the theoretical XP will be calculated).[42 Paris only]

**Do you consider getting a RNCP certification through a VAE (Validation of Acquired Experience) process?**The VAE process is accessible to 42 Paris Alumni. Depending on the remaining projects you need to validate to obtain the RNCP certification, the Pedago will evaluate if you need to go through a VAE jury (recognition of your professional experience) or through a regular certification session. Please read the VAE section for more information.**How often do you complete projects?**If you haven’t validated a project in months, most probably because you are working or less committed, consider becoming an Alumni. The career and networking opportunities available on the Alumni platform are best suited to professionals.**How involved are you in the school life?**If you are highly involved, the Alumni community will expand your horizons while maintaining the same access you had as a student (you can still complete projects but won’t increase level). If you are mildly or hardly involved, the Alumni community might inspire you to reconnect with your peers and give your career a boost.

### What is the process to obtain a RNCP certification as an Alumni?

A RNCP certification is a state-certified qualification recognized throughout France but also across Europe. Two RNCP certifications are delivered by 42:

- RNCP level 6, equivalent to a bachelor's degree (Bac+3)
- RNCP level 7, equivalent to a master's degree (Bac+5)

RNCP certifications are available in the following campuses: Angoulême, Antwerp/Brussels, Barcelona, Beirut, Istanbul/Kocaeli, Kuala Lumpur, Lausanne, Le Havre, Lyon, Madrid, Málaga, Mulhouse, Nice, Paris, Perpignan, Prague, Seoul, Singapore, Urduliz, Vienna, Warsaw [as of March 2026, subject to change].

How it works:

STEP 1: Check that your campus is able to deliver RNCP certifications (see the list above). Contact your Pedago team in case of doubt.

STEP 2: Check your eligibility for RNCP 6 or RNCP 7 with the Grand Cursus Checker.

STEP 3: Follow the procedure based on your situation

**You meet all the requirements**: Contact your campus Pedago to register to the next RNCP jury. You will go through a regular RNCP certification jury (identical to students).**You are only missing a few projects**: You will go through a regular RNCP certification jury (identical to students) after completing the remaining projects as an Alumni. Since your level is frozen as an Alumni, we will use theoretical XP earned on new projects (and theoretical "new" level) so that you meet the eligibility requirements. Your campus Pedago will guide you through this process; contact them directly should you wish to access the campus or require guidance.

Note: If you need to validate one of the company projects (Work Experience I & II, Startup Experience), a professional contract (fixed or permanent) is necessary as 42 can no longer provide you with an internship agreement. You will need to request your Pedago to force your registration (work experiences are blocked by default for Alumni). Caution: we do not validate work experience retroactively so the sooner you register for one of the work experience, the sooner you will complete the necessary 6 months in a company and meet the RNCP requirements.

**You are an external or a 42 Paris Alumni who do not meet the requirements**: You need to go through a VAE process (Validation of Acquired Experience). Please read the VAE section for more information.**You are an Alumni (not from 42 Paris) who do not meet the requirements**: At the moment it is not possible to obtain a RNCP certification on your campus if you do not meet the requirements.

Caution: If you are a STUDENT in the process of obtaining a RNCP certification, make sure that you have completed all the mandatory steps (required level, projects, jury evaluation) before becoming an Alumni.

### Is VAE (Validation of Acquired Experience) possible as an Alumni?

VAE sometimes translates in English to APEL (Accreditation of Prior Experiential Learning).

As a **42 Paris** Alumni and if you meet the requirements below, you can initiate a VAE process to obtain the RNCP 6 or 7 certification:

- have one year of experience related to the desired qualification
- speak French fluently (but the application file can be submitted in French or in English)
- attend the jury in person in Paris, France
- sustain the cost of the VAE process (250€) -- the price shown includes the 50% Alumni discount

Caution: VAE is only available on the 42 Paris campus.

How it works: Find more information about VAE and register for the next info session at this link: https://42.fr/le-campus-de-paris/diplome-informatique/vae/. Should you have any questions, email vae@42paris.fr.

Note: If your level requires you to validate only a few projects to obtain the RNCP certification, you might be able to go through a regular certification session. In that case, refer to the RNCP section above.

### I was hired and could not finish my “professional experience” project. Can I become an Alumni?

To become an Alumni, you need to have completed your company project (work experience, internship, apprenticeship ...). Turn in your video and/or complete your pending projects before your Alumnization date to earn extra levels.

### I am a 42.zip/Pole Emploi student, can I become an Alumni? [France only]

No, only 42 and 42cursus students are eligible for the Alumni status.

## REFUSING TO BE AN ALUMNI

### What if I don't want to become an Alumni?

**By default**: After 8 months of inactivity after completing the Common Core (no XP increase), you will automatically become an Alumni (at 11:00 PM on the scheduled date). An Automatic Alumnization is not reversible. Having set deadlines allows for improved time management and objective completion - it will help you go as far as you can in your 42 training. Plus, if you are no longer studying, the Alumni status is more suited to your professional needs.**You are about to be Alumnized but would like to finish projects**(for personal reasons or in case of RNCP certification): email your campus’ Pedago to request a Pushback (allow time before your deadline expires!). Your campus Pedago can extend your Alumnization date by up to 8 additional months from the present date. Pushback conditions may vary by campus.**You are no longer a student and have no interest in the 42 community**: You can request personal data erasure at this link: https://signin.intra.42.fr/legal/terms/5 After that, you won’t be linked to 42 anymore and 42 will no longer have data about you. However, you will still be able to request a grade transcript or a certificate of completion from 42 for 50 years.

### I have been Alumnized without my consent. Can I reverse it?

Alumnization is not reversible.

Note that prior to an Automatic Alumnization, you will receive 2 reminder emails: 4 months, then 1 month before your Alumnization. Both reminder emails display your scheduled Alumnization date. You can also monitor your Alumnization date from your intranet homepage at all times (intra v3 only).

After 8 months of inactivity (no XP increase) and without action from you after two reminders, we consider that it is very unlikely that you will resume your training at 42. Rejoice, there are many advantages to being an Alumni, see Benefits.

### What is the Alumni platform? How to access it?

The Alumni platform (42alumni.org) is an online platform dedicated to Alumni to network with other 42 Alumni, follow 42 news, organize all kinds of events, post or apply to job offers, etc. It is also possible to join and create regional and interest groups.

### What is the Alumni Shop? How to access it?

An online store exists for Alumni who would like to get their hands on exclusive 42 goodies (t-shirt, sweatshirt, cap, mugs, etc.). New products are regularly added to the shop: shop.42alumni.org.

After completing the Alumni Survey (direct link: https://profile.intra.42.fr/alumni), you'll receive a personal discount code to use on the online shop.

### What is Credly? How does it work?

Our partner platform Credly allows you to claim official 42 badges and get 42 certificates once you become an Alumni or obtain a RNCP certification (delivered by 42).

The minimum requirement to obtain an Alumni badge is Level 9.

Share your badge(s) on LinkedIn to showcase your credentials to your professional network. Provide your authenticated 42 certificate to future employers, government agencies, and other stakeholders in case of background checks, visa approvals, and more.

By scanning the QR code on the 42 certificate, (future) employers can directly access Credly to verify the document’s authenticity and view detailed information about the skills acquired.

Credly is only accessible to Alumni and RNCP-holders.

**How it works:**

1- Upon Alumnization (or shortly after obtaining your RNCP certification, delivered by 42), you will receive an email asking you to claim your badge on Credly. Claiming your badge is optional, you can do it at any time.

2- Create your account on Credly. Your information will be automatically retrieved from your 42 intra account.

3- Claim your badge. You can own several badges: your final Level and/or RNCP. If you need a certificate, Credly allows you to download it at any time.

**Frequent questions:**

How do I get started? Watch this tutorial.

What if I already own a Credly account linked to another institution?

- If the email address is the same for both Credly accounts, badges would appear under the same account.
- At any time, you can add a secondary email to your Credly account. That way, if you are ever issued badges to different emails, they will appear on the same account: learn more.
- If you have 2 different accounts on Credly, under 2 different emails, you can merge your accounts together: learn more.

- I do not want to share my data with Credly/external parties
- Activating your Credly account is optional. If you do not want to activate your account, simply ignore Credly's activation email.
- If you have activated your Credly account, you can decide to "hide" your badge and certificate for some time (e.g. when your job search is over).
- If you wish for some information to be removed from your Credly profile, email alumni@42.fr.
- Be mindful when sharing your badge and certificate with external parties.

I need to amend my certificate (spelling errors, missing capital letters…): email alumni@42.fr. There’s no need to contact Credly support, as they cannot update data on behalf of 42 due to privacy regulations.

For further assistance, visit Credly Help Center.

Please note that RNCP certifications delivered by Ynov are not eligible to Credly.

### Do I still benefit from student rates on software licenses?

As an Alumni, you are technically no longer a student so you don’t benefit from student discounts or free license anymore.

## ALUMNI SUPPORT

### What is the Alumni Survey?

Once you become an Alumni, 42 will invite you to complete a survey about your professional situation every year (only the first three years). The results will be used for pedagogical certifications and very useful to keep the 42 curriculum in line with the students’ and job market expectations.

General statistics (i.e. employment rate) will also be shared with the 42 community.

As a thank you for filling the Alumni Survey, you will receive a personal discount code to use on the 42 Alumni Shop.

### What is the 42 Certificate of Completion? How can I obtain it?

The 42 Certificate of Completion is a document issued by 42, which is common to all campuses. It proves the last level you reached at 42. Caution: this is not a diploma.

This certificate is in English only, contact your campus to obtain it in your local language.

This certificate will be accessible through our partner platform Credly **after you become an Alumni** - see What is Credly?. If you are an Alumni (as stated in your intra) and haven’t received your Credly credentials, email alumni@42.fr.

### Can I change campus as an Alumni?

Campus transfers are not permitted for Alumni.

### I can't access my intra account anymore, how to retrieve it?

If you haven't logged in to your intra account for over 3 years, your intra account will be erased in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR). Unfortunately, in that case, it is not possible to retrieve your account.

You will receive three email notifications before your intra account is deleted: two months prior, two weeks before, and on the day of deletion. Simply logging in once a year at https://signin.intra.42.fr is sufficient to prevent account deletion.

Email alumni@42.fr if you have a doubt.

### I can't find or create my account on the Alumni platform, what is the procedure?

The Alumni platform is only available to Alumni. Make sure the tag 'Alumni' is showing on your intra before proceeding.

To access the Alumni platform, you first need to activate your Alumni benefits on 42's intranet. Here is how to do it: 1- Log in to your intra and follow this link: https://profile.intra.42.fr/alumni 2- If you are officially an Alumni, the Alumni survey will appear. Complete it to activate your benefits. 3- Once this is done, you will receive your login details for the Alumni platform via email.

### I can't access the global '42born2code' Slack, how to retrieve it?

If you can't access the '42born2code' global Slack channel, try first to change your password. The email used remains your 42 your student address.

If this doesn't work, your account on the ‘42born2code’ Slack might have been deactivated or erased due to General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR). If you wish to be added again (provided you meet the requirements), email alumni@42.fr.

### What about my personal data?

After 3 years of inactivity as an Alumni, you will receive account deletion warnings via email, in accordance with the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

- To keep your intranet account and the services associated with it (student address, GitHub/cloud accounts, Alumni platform...), you need to log in to your account at: https://signin.intra.42.fr.
- To permanently delete your access to the intranet, no action is required from you.

You may at any time exercise your rights regarding the processing of your data: https://signin.intra.42.fr/legal/terms/5

The request for erasure of your data concerns all our services. After data erasure, you will no longer be able to enjoy your Alumni benefits.
