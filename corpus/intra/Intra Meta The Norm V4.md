# Intra Meta The Norm V4

The Norm

Latest version is 4.1 (Jan 10th, 2025).

Various remarks about the Norm:

- Why do we have the Norm? => please read the Norm pdf, it does contain a full page with explanations on that.
- Why there are differences between the Norm pdf and the Norminette? => it's easy to understand that some requests from the norm cannot automatically be controlled, like "english names for vars and functions". Also, the high flexibility of the C coding language make difficult to extend the Norminette to all possible syntaxes.
- What should we do during an evaluation? => during an evaluation, the Norminette prevails on controlled items (unless specific local directives on your campus). We would have to double all evaluation durations if we wanted to fully check the Norm using the pdf, and many conflicts would raise (that's what history tells us, this has been tested years ago). So to keep evaluations focused enough on the project features, you only use the Norminette for items that it controls. As an evaluator, if you notice them you can indicate that some points of the Norm, not controlled by the Norminette, are not respected, and hit the "Norm" flag. Such items are marked with a
`(*)`

in the pdf.
