# Intra Meta Project translations

*Important note*

The intranet may offer translated subject PDFs and evaluation sheets in various languages. These translations are the result of a community effort and are completed asynchronously. As a result, you may occasionally notice small differences between the English version — the only one maintained by 42 Central — and your preferred translated version.

Please keep the following in mind:

- If a translated PDF exists and only differs from the English version by a minor version (e.g., English is version 4.2 and translated is version 4.1), it will still be made available. In that case, there may be minor differences between the PDFs, but the expected outcome for the evaluation remains unchanged.
- If a significant change is made to the subject and/or the evaluation sheet, the major version of the subject will be updated. This will temporarily remove translated versions from the intranet until the community has translated the latest version.
- If you notice important differences between the English version and the version in your language, the English version takes precedence. You may report this to your local staff, who will forward the issue to the central team.

Thank you!
