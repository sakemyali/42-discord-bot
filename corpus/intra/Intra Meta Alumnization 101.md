# Intra Meta Alumnization 101

⚠️ For Pushback or Alumnization status inquiries, contact your campus Pedago team directly. Emails to other addresses will not be processed. ⚠️

### 2 ways to become Alumni:

**Automatic Alumnization**: Inactive for 8 months (no XP or company project) or after 4.5 years in the Advanced Curriculum (post-Common Core).**Manual request**: Students can decide to become Alumni anytime via the Alumni Launchpad (direct link: https://profile.intra.42.fr/alumni).

💡 Students are eligible for the Alumni status only after completing the Common Core.

### Rules to be aware of:

**Inactivity**: Students are automatically alumnized after 8 months of inactivity (no XP or company project) or after 4.5 years in the Advanced Curriculum (post-Common Core).**Pushback**: Students may request a delay (up to 8 months) for personal reasons, subject to campus approval. Pushback conditions may vary by campus.**Company Project**: Alumnization is postponed for the length of the internship or apprenticeship.**Hard Deadline**: The 4.5-year limit is strict, with exceptions only for significant issues like long-term illness or military service.

🚧 You may receive reminder emails during a company project if your internship or apprenticeship ends near the Alumnization deadline. These reminders alert you that you'll have limited time after finishing the company project to earn XP before the Automatic Alumnization.

### How it works:

- If you're inactive (no XP or company project) for
**4 months**, you get an email reminder. - After
**7 months**of inactivity, a second email warning is sent. - At
**8 months**, you automatically become Alumni. This process pauses if you start a company project, and you can request a delay ("Pushback") for personal reasons, but the maximum time in the Advanced Curriculum is 4.5 years.

🚨 Alumnization occurs

at 11:00 PM on the scheduled datein your local timezone (e.g. if your deadline is January 10, you will be Alumnized at 11:00 PM of that day).

### Frequent questions:

**When does the countdown towards Alumnization start after Common Core?**When ‘Transcendance’ and Exam rank 6 are completed.**Where can I see how much time is left before my Alumnization?**An Alumnization countdown appears on your intranet homepage (intra v3 only - if you are on intra v2, switch to intra v3 to see your deadline). You will also receive reminder emails as your deadline approaches, including your exact Alumnization date.**Is there a limit to the number of Pushbacks allowed per student?**Pushbacks are at the discretion of each campus, conditions may vary by campus. However, they must fit within the 4.5-year curriculum limit.**Can I cancel a Pushback?**No.**At what time exactly will I be Alumnized?**Alumnization occurs at 11:00 PM on the scheduled date in your local timezone (e.g. if your deadline is January 10, you will be Alumnized at 11:00 PM of that day).**I have been Alumnized before I could push my project. Can I get additional time?**No. An Alumnization is not reversible. It follows a 8-month period during which you could have validated your project or requested a Pushback. While you can still push your project as an Alumni, you will not earn XP.**What is an Alumni ban?**Following a disciplinary sanction or exclusion, a campus may declare a student ineligible for Alumni status (no access to Alumni benefits or 42 resources). The student will receive an email notification of this decision and can appeal by contacting the campus Pedago.
