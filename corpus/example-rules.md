## Submitting a project

A project is submitted via the intra. Each project has a deadline and a
required peer-evaluation step before the final mark is recorded. Submitting
without a peer evaluation does not count as a finished project.

## Peer evaluations

Evaluations happen between two students (corrector and corrected) at a
booked time slot. The corrector inspects the submitted code, runs it,
checks against the project's evaluation rubric, and posts feedback through
the intra interface. Both parties must be present for the eval to count.

If the corrector cancels with less than one hour notice, the corrected
student does not lose evaluation points and can rebook the slot.

## Black Hole

The Black Hole is a deadline by which a student in the main curriculum
must finish their next project. Each finished project pushes the Black
Hole date further out. If the Black Hole date passes without progress,
the student is removed from the main curriculum.

The Black Hole date is visible on each student's intra profile under the
cursus tab.

## Freeze

A freeze is a temporary pause in Black Hole progression that staff may
grant for documented reasons (medical, mandatory military service, family
emergencies). A freeze must be requested in writing through the intra
ticketing system and approved by staff before it takes effect.

## Code of conduct

Students are expected to treat each other and staff with respect. Reports
of harassment or harmful behaviour can be sent confidentially via the
ticketing system or directly to staff. All reports are taken seriously
and investigated.
